# Accessibility
Definition of done: semantic HTML, keyboard operation, visible focus, meaningful labels, screen-reader understandable forms/errors, colour-independent meaning, sufficient contrast, reduced motion, responsive layout, sensible touch targets, heading structure, accessible chart summaries.
Cognitive accessibility: predictable navigation, concise instructions, low information density, clear back/exit routes, no unnecessary urgency/punishment.
Automated checks do not replace keyboard/mobile review. Accessibility failures are P1 unless more severe.
